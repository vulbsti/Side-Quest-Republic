# Humour rubric

You are judging lines and scenes from Side Quest Republic, a game about a Gen Z protest day at Jantar Mantar and
Connaught Place, Delhi. The audience is the people who comment on protest reels from @peeinghuman and similar pages.
Score how many of these would make that audience laugh or type 😂, from 1 to 5.

What already made that audience laugh (real lines and comments from reels the owner collected):
- "Aap party mein bula rahe ho mujhe?" (a detainee to the woman constable walking her to the bus)
- "Mujhe akele ko identify karke detain kiya hai, poori bus mein koi aur nahi"
- "Koi tear gas ka intezaam nahi kiye? Water cannon hi chala dijiye."
- Comments: "Season 1 pro player", "Protest no, adventure yes", "perks of being skinny", "bro just hijack the bus",
  "Absolute cinema", "This is jantar mantar entertainment 2.0", "most expensive player in the next PKL season"
- The rival game's copy: "Delhi Police ki free van seva. Released in... 2", "Ex Minister: lost his convoy, here to 'clarify'",
  "Wheelchair warrior: ramp ya na ho, aa raha hai"

The mechanisms that work: goal reversal, scale collision (mummy's call beats the lathi charge), a literal service
request to riot police, borrowed confidence, the officer who doesn't know how to react, a callback to something
the player did. Specific Delhi detail beats generic jokes.

What does not work: lectures and safety advice; an English sentence with "bhai" bolted on; explaining the joke;
a character saying something just to state the plot; jokes any city could have.

Scores:
1. Nobody laughs. Reads like instructions, plot or a lesson.
2. A smile at one or two lines; most is filler.
3. Several lines would get a 😂 in comments; scenes have one real beat.
4. Most lines land; scenes build to a punchline; it sounds like the reels.
5. People would screen-record this and post it.

Answer with JSON: score (1-5, halves allowed), why (two sentences, quote the best and the worst line), fix (the
single change that would raise the score most).
