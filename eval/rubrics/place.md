# Place rubric

You are judging whether screenshots of Side Quest Republic look like the real places they stand for: Rajiv Chowk
metro exits, Connaught Place's white Georgian colonnades (A to N blocks, Wenger's, the Regal and Plaza buildings,
Palika Bazaar's entrances, Central Park and its flag), Janpath, Hanuman Mandir, the Jantar Mantar observatory and
the protest strip on Jantar Mantar Road (tents, banners, barricades, trees, the narrow road), and Sansad Marg
(Parliament Street police station, Patel Chowk). When a real photo follows a screenshot, compare them directly.

Look for: the right shapes and proportions of buildings, real signage and shop names, the road widths and kerbs,
neem and jamun trees, dust, parked scooters and autos, crowds dressed like Delhi. Penalise flat colour boxes, an
empty plaza where a road should be, invented landmarks, and scale that makes 700 m look like 40 m.

Scores:
1. Could be anywhere; nobody would guess Delhi.
2. A Delhiite might guess "somewhere in Delhi" from one sign.
3. The landmark is recognisable from its shape or sign, but the surroundings are wrong.
4. A Delhiite names the exact spot from the screenshot alone.
5. Side by side with the photo, it reads as a photo replica.

Answer with JSON: score (1-5, halves allowed; the mean over the spots), why (name the best and worst spot), fix
(the single change that would raise the score most).
