# The afternoon remembers: whole-day content

24 original fictional encounters span a 1200-second day. Four baseline choices per encounter include nonparticipation. Four encounters add a fifth opportunity gated by earlier actions, for 100 authored choices total. Twenty textual callbacks across eleven scenes explicitly recall earlier events. All encounters carry exact world locations and explicit helpful/disruptive/neutral test-policy tags. Every scene has an independent expiry outcome. The unattended day remains active; players cannot be everywhere.

## Dramatic structure
Morning (0–330): comedy, consent, ordinary work, borrowing, promises, neighbourhood needs. Midday (230–700): editing, negotiation ambiguity, deliveries, political amplification and callbacks. Rupture (550–1000): rumours, provocation, communication failure, access, injury. Crisis (810–1170): conflicting accounts, limited concessions, regrouping and a chosen final responsibility. Aftermath (1080–1200): repair, listening and competing histories. Overlap is deliberate.

## Social causality
Characters have livelihood and institutional obligations as well as personal motives. Officers can provide assistance while operating within restrictive instructions. An unsupported allegation is distinct from a verified observation. Political pressure is represented by fictional Jan Pragati Front intermediaries; no scene claims a named real party committed a covert act. The ambiguous delivery is never silently transformed into proof of weapons.

The four promises are speaker_care, water_return, shop_route, check_nikhil. Later scenes allow delivery, honest inability, neglect and repair. Evidence stores observations, rumours store claims with uncertainty. The protagonist can contribute to escalation, and the effects acknowledge costs to others rather than treating violence as a consequence-free score reward.

## Presentation
Every scene carries original in-engine camera, caption, music and voice-style metadata. This is direction metadata, not produced voice recordings or licensed reel footage. Morning timing supports humour; injury and crisis use quiet or tense treatments without comic stings. Reels must distinguish actual player actions from public interpretations.

## Acceptance
Run idle, helpful, disruptive and inconsistent seeded days. Expect scenes to expire, promises to resolve or remain broken, claims to retain their sources, and the climax/aftermath to reflect accumulated actions. Inspect candidate decisions and rejected alternatives. Check that missed scenes remain missed, characters cannot know unwitnessed claims, and reload preserves clocks, choices and promises.
