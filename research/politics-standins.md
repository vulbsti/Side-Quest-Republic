# How parties, street groups and police actually behaved (2025–2026), for the RUP / DRS / LJM stand-ins

Owner decision (19 Oct): use fictional stand-ins that "very closely reflect reality". Invent the names, but make the flags, colours, slogans, vehicles and tactics instantly recognisable. Never use a real organisation or politician name in the game.

This file covers what sources say happened, so that writers can borrow the *behaviour*. Each claim is tagged:
- **[R]** reported as fact by the source;
- **[A]** an allegation or claim by one side that the source does not verify;
- **[I]** my inference or a game suggestion.

Sources were read on 2026-10-20 through a summarising fetch tool unless marked *(search title only)*. Several big outlets (NDTV, TOI, The Guardian, India Today, DW, The Wire body text) were blocked or rate-limited, so coverage leans on agency copy, ThePrint, Newslaundry, National Herald, Free Press Journal, WION and fact-checkers. Both pro-movement (National Herald, BT News) and pro-government (OpIndia) outlets were read for framing. Neither is treated as neutral.

## 1. The ruling party → **RUP (Rashtriya Uday Party)**

**What the ruling side actually did:**
- **Label the protesters.**
  - The education minister called them "the B-team of terrorists. The people who were rejected by the public have come back in another disguise." [R, quote] [Kashmir Reader](https://kashmirreader.com/2026/06/26/rahul-demands-apology-from-pradhan-over-terrorists-remark-on-student-protesters/)
  - He also accused them of "acting against the nation". [R] [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/20/police-attack-cockroach-activists-as-thousands-march-on-indian-parliament)
  - A 15 Aug prime-ministerial speech used the term "Dimagi Naxal". [R] [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2026/10/08/from-5-lakh-to-10-lakh-social-media-claims-fuel-counter-mobilisation-ahead-of-cjp-protest)
  - Pro-government media framed the October protest as "unlawful agitation", "unauthorised protest" and "created anarchy", and tied a student leader to "violent agitations" and "Nepal". [R, framing] [OpIndia live](https://www.opindia.com/2026/10/live-updates-ground-reports-cjp-protest-jantar-mantar-gyanesh-kumar-eci/)
- **Foreign hand and outside agitators.**
  - Ladakh: the Home Ministry said the leader "misled" people through "Arab Spring-style" and Nepal Gen Z references. His NGO's foreign-funding licence was cancelled the next day and a CBI inquiry opened. [R] [HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/09/25/india-4-dead-in-protests-over-ladakh-statehood)
  - In 2026, foreign-funding allegations circulated and were dismissed by the MEA. [R, via Wikipedia]
  - A police source alleged "Pakistan-based handlers" doctored an audio clip. [A] [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/15-fake-social-media-posts-rebutted-by-delhi-police-since-cjp-protest-1973058)
- **Small concession plus selective talks.**
  - The PM announced fast-track courts for paper-leak cases ("Those who try to harm the future of our youth will not be spared"). [R] [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/viral/protest-hai-ya-holi-party-patna-student-protesters-ask-police-for-water-cannon-amid-heat-dance-in-the-spray-video)
  - The government invited two activists to talks with a senior minister on the day of the march. [R] [Al Jazeera]
  - The movement ended after a joint press conference with ruling-party leaders, and the opposition questioned the deal. [R] [Wikipedia], [Open](https://openthemagazine.com/india/delhi-police-identifies-2873-people-with-criminal-records-at-student-protest-sites-using-facial-recognition)
- **Workers on the street.** In Patna on 22 Jul, ruling-party workers marched to gherao the opposition party's office. Both sides allegedly used lathis and stones, and each called it "a pre-planned attempt", a "deliberate conspiracy". [R/A] [FPJ (IANS)](https://www.freepressjournal.in/bihar/bjp-congress-workers-clash-in-patna-amid-student-protests-several-injured-video)
- **"Goons" and the stone truck.**
  - The movement's founder alleged ruling-party "goons", not students, pelted stones, and asked who parked a stone-laden truck at the site. He said police told him it was a municipal (MCD) truck.
  - He also claimed he was pressured to join the party.
  - The party did not respond. [A] [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/bjp-goons-not-students-pelted-stones-at-police-cjp-founder-abhijeet-dipke-video)
- **Online cadre.** Templated posts amplified counter-mobilisation. Accounts carried the bio "मोदी का परिवार" or "IT Cell wala". Posts tagged state party youth wings. [R, with no official link established] [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2026/10/08/from-5-lakh-to-10-lakh-social-media-claims-fuel-counter-mobilisation-ahead-of-cjp-protest)
- **Inner conflict is real.**
  - A former state party chief publicly asked "What kept you waiting?" about the delay in talks. [R, headline] [HT](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/what-kept-you-waiting-annamalai-questions-bjp-centre-over-talks-with-neet-protest-cjp-jantar-mantar-dharmendra-pradhan-101784774032133.html) *(search/Wikipedia citation only)*
  - On 10 Oct the daughter of a former ruling-party MLA was among the detainees. [R] [ThePrint live]

## 2. Hindutva-style street groups → **DRS (Dharma Raksha Sena)**

**What happened:**
- **Debut-day attack (6 Jun).** Student groups alleged supporters of the RSS-linked camp physically attacked protesters at the first sit-in, including with "cockroach spray". The headline quote: "RSS Will Not Deter Us." [A] [The Wire](https://thewire.in/rights/rss-will-not-deter-us-say-student-bodies-on-alleged-attacks-at-cjps-debut-jantar-mantar-protest) (metadata only; body not readable)
- **Ink on the stage (18 Jul).** A self-described men's rights activist threw blue ink at the founder. She raised religious slogans as police took her away. [R] [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/woman-hurls-ink-at-cjp-founder-dipke-he-remains-unfazed-says-blue-is-my-colour/) Wikipedia describes her as chanting Hindutva slogans.
- **Pre-protest hype (7–8 Oct).**
  - Posts claimed "5 lakh" to "10 lakh" VHP/RSS workers would come, and that "Bajrang Dal is moving!" and "NOW THE REAL FUN BEGINS!" (#FreeStreetsOfNewDelhi).
  - Posts called protesters "cockroaches" to be "taught a lesson".
  - One showed a man holding a wooden stick, with a convoy leaving a mall at 10 am.
  - A youth group promised "a befitting reply".
  - Old saffron-scarf footage from August was passed off as new.
  - The organisations themselves denied it ("misleading, malicious propaganda").
  - On the day, ThePrint's live blog records no actual counter-protest. [R] [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2026/10/08/from-5-lakh-to-10-lakh-social-media-claims-fuel-counter-mobilisation-ahead-of-cjp-protest), [ThePrint live](https://theprint.in/india/jantar-mantar-delhi-cjp-protest-live-updates/3066985/)
- **Older pattern at the same site (2021, outside the window).** Communal slogans raised at Jantar Mantar led to a former party spokesperson and a street-group chief being detained and booked. That chief later posted an online video talking about surrender. [R, older] [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/jantar-mantar-hate-slogan-case-accused-hindu-raksha-dal-chief-talks-of-surrender-in-online-video-304237) *(search title only)*

**Observed shape [I, from the above]:**
- The leadership deny everything while the online foot soldiers hype numbers and sticks.
- Actual turnout is smaller than the hype.
- The damage comes from single actors (ink, spray, scuffles) and from rumour, more than from a marching mass.

**DRS in the game [I]:**
- **Look:** saffron scarves and tilak, motorbikes in a convoy, one bike with a megaphone horn, a bhagwa flag on a bamboo pole. One man carries a lathi wrapped in a flag.
- **Slogans:** the invented chant "Desh ke gaddaron ko…" should trail off unfinished. Writers should not complete a real hate slogan. Use "Jai Shri…"-style openers cut off by a horn, or invented lines such as "Sena aayi, safai aayi!"
- **Behaviour:**
  - The phone rumour says "5 lakh", and six bikes arrive.
  - The foot soldier on a bike wants a viral video. The leader on the phone says "koi haath nahi uthayega" while a teenager does it anyway.
  - Single-actor disruption: paint, spray, a shove at the mic.
  - They film the students more than they chant.
- **Inner conflict:** the leader needs deniability ("humara aadmi nahi tha"). A young member came for the bike ride and leaves when the tear gas starts.

## 3. The opposition → **LJM (Lok Jan Morcha)**

**What happened:**
- **Court the cameras by getting detained.**
  - The Leader of Opposition and others were detained near the PM's residence on 22 Jul and released. [R] [France 24 via Wikipedia]
  - On 10 Oct a chief minister, a former Delhi chief minister and a sitting state chief minister (with MPs) were each detained. One party said its leader was "brutally taken into custody", and another called it "murder of democracy". [R] [ThePrint live]
  - From another city, a national leader said "Arrest us all… We are surrounding you, and we are not going to back down." [R] [ThePrint live]
- **Brand the movement for themselves.**
  - The main opposition party launched a 40-day "Chhatron Ki Goonj" campaign ending in a "Delhi Chalo" march. [R] [Kashmir Reader](https://kashmirreader.com/2026/06/26/rahul-demands-apology-from-pradhan-over-terrorists-remark-on-student-protesters/)
  - The INDIA bloc called a march against "Vote Chori". [R] [ThePrint live]
  - A UP party leader used "three stages in Delhi for one big gain". [R, headline] [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/akhilesh-yadav-samajwadi-party-delhi-protest-strategy-jantar-mantar-parliament-pm-residence-up-election-youth-2954219-2026-07-23) *(blocked; headline only)*
- **Amplify police excess.** Party handles shared the alleged drug-plant threat video ("Raising one's voice is not a crime"), and a former MP filed a police complaint against the slapping officer. [R] [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/will-destroy-your-life-viral-video-shows-mumbai-cop-allegedly-threatening-detained-students-with-false-drug-case-police-orders-inquiry), [WION](https://www.wionews.com/india-news/lost-his-cool-delhi-cop-who-slapped-woman-during-parliament-march-removed-from-protest-site-1784783693383)

OpIndia labelled organisers by their party links ("AAP youth leader", "AAP supporter"), which shows how party association gets used *against* a movement. [R]

**LJM in the game [I]:**
- **Look:** white-blue-green flags, ceiling-fan symbol, Gandhi caps on the older workers. The leader arrives in a convoy at 13:00 with a photographer walking backwards in front of him.
- **Behaviour:**
  - Wants the stage and the mic.
  - Gets "detained" with a smile, at a time that suits the evening bulletin.

  - Fights RUP workers over who started it.
- **Inner conflict:** a local LJM worker genuinely brought food and water. The leader wants a photo with Mira, and Mira knows the photo will be used against the students.

## 5. The "outsiders / provocateurs" pattern

Both sides use the same accusation, which is exactly why the game's provocateur mechanic must stay ambiguous until filmed.

- **The movement says:**
  - Ruling-party "goons" threw the stones, and a truck of stones was parked at the site [A].
  - Plainclothes men did the beating [A].
  - Fringe groups were being mobilised "nationwide" [A, attributed by an organiser to an unnamed party insider].
  - Sources: [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/bjp-goons-not-students-pelted-stones-at-police-cjp-founder-abhijeet-dipke-video), [Newslaundry](https://www.newslaundry.com/2026/10/08/from-5-lakh-to-10-lakh-social-media-claims-fuel-counter-mobilisation-ahead-of-cjp-protest).
- **The state says:**
  - There were thousands of people with criminal records in the crowd [A].
  - "Pakistan-based handlers" were behind fakes [A].
  - The protesters are a "B-team of terrorists" or foreign-funded.
  - On 10 Oct, 25 to 30 protesters jumped barricades near Akashvani Bhawan and threw stones, injuring two police [A, PTI/officials]. Sources: [South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/beyond-south/the-road-to-jantar-mantar-a-day-inside-indias-sealed-capital/), ThePrint live.
- **Pre-protest bait:** a clip of a self-described supporter saying "Nepal will be made" and urging people to buy "pepper spray, sticks" circulated before 6 Jun. Critics used it, and the founder replied "I believe in the Constitution of India more than anything else." [R] [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/india/we-have-to-turn-india-into-nepal-cockroach-janta-party-supporter-video-triggers-outrage-before-june-6-protest-in-delhi)

**Game rule [I]:**
- The three SUV men have physical tells (new shoes, earpiece, helmet in a bag, no placard, filming the police rather than the stage).
- Until someone films them with those tells, the ticker blames "students".
- Once a clip exists, the ticker splits: the RUP feed says "criminal elements", Zoya says "who brought them?".
- Never confirm in-game who sent them. Let the SUV be the evidence.
